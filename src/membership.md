### LGCC Membership fees 2026-27

- Full Adult Member: £50
- Adult Playing Member whose Mother Club is not Letham Grange (*): £20
- Non-playing Adult (N): £15
- Junior (Y) 18-21 years: £15
- Junior (J) under 18 years: £15
- Gateway Club Members: £15

Note: If subscription is not received by 1st November, the member will be excluded from playing any further club or club-sponsored games until fees are paid in full.

### ICE FEES FOR 2026-2027 SEASON

- FCCC Yearly Membership Fee is £25 or pay additional £3 per game. The Membership Fee can be paid online through the new fis.scot website.
- Weekdays – daytime: £13.00 non-member £15.00
- Weekdays – evening: £17.00 non-member £20.00
- Weekends – All times: £17.00 non-member £20.00
- Practice Ice: £12.00 non-member £15.00
- FCCC Junior fees: £12.00 (LGCC will subsidise Juniors Ice by £7.00 per game)

### Letham Grange Curling Club information

- Club leagues - Bank of Scotland, Contractors Cup, Spring League.
- Club Bonspiels – Pairs, Doubles, Opening, New Year, Closing, Crichton and Men vs Women.
- Points – All members and friends welcome

The club is also heavily involved with the Province, Area and Ice Rink competitions such as;

- Dewar KO; Ferburn League; Points; and Indoor and/or Outdoor Bonspiel.
- Indoor Bonspiel; Grant Trophy; Crichton Tankard; Inter Province Bonspiel.
- Super League, Agricar League; Seniors League; Bonspiels for Opening, Closing and Charity; Inter Club matches; Rink Points.

Province, Area and Ice Rink competition dates and events are in our yearly syllabus

The club also runs many social events such as:-

- Fun days – Meeting up for a social event like bowling.
- Away days – Playing at other ice rinks in national competitions
- Dinners – Attend dinners when visiting tour come to Forfar. Dinner as part of our Prizegiving.
- Prize ceremonies – Annual presentation of prizes

### Volunteers

Letham Grange Curling Club is self funded and run by the members volunteering to being on the Committee. We are always grateful for assistance in running the club from any of its members and especially from the parents/carers of new junior members. This could involve anything from lifts to competitions , to being on the committee or becoming a coach or official.
If you are interested in volunteering please contact the President or Secretary:-

President - Dave Piggot Contact- president@lethamgrangecc.org.uk
Secretary - Brian McArtney Contact- secretary@lethamgrangecc.org.uk

### Child wellbeing and protection

Letham Grange Curling Club adheres to the highest standards of child wellbeing and protection, adhering to the Child wellbeing protection policy set out by Scottish curling . All our coaches and helpers have undergone PVG checks and the club has its own Child wellbeing protection officer
CWPO - Jane McArtney Contact - wellbeing@lethamgrangecc.org.uk
